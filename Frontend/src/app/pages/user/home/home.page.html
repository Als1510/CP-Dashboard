FAQs

Can I edit or update my coding profiles later?

Yes, you can edit your coding profiles at any time from your profile settings. Click the "Edit" button next to the profile you want to update.

What information can I find on my dashboard?

Your dashboard displays coding round details, upcoming contests, your rankings, and coding statistics for selected platforms like Codechef, Codeforces etc.

How do I track my progress in coding contests?

Our dashboard automatically tracks your contest performance and provides insights into your progress, including ratings, rankings, and solved problems.

Is my coding profile data secure on CP Dashboard?

We take data privacy seriously. Your coding profile data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not share your data with third parties.

What information is visible to other users?

By default, your coding profiles are visible only to you.
